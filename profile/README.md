## What we build

We ship small, focused tools that solve one painful problem for indie hackers, founders, and small teams. Every product here is open source under MIT, documented for AI-native workflows, and shipped from a single founder operation.

## Two companies, one operation

**[HouseofMVPs](https://houseofmvps.com)** is the build studio. We take validated ideas and ship MVPs in days, then iterate based on real user behavior. Everything we publish here came out of building HouseofMVPs products in the open.

**[Kailxlabs](https://www.kailxlabs.co)** is the specialty growth agency. We work with cash-pay clinics, law firms, premium home services, and cosmetic practices on AI search visibility, citation engineering, and conversion design. We engage 10 clients at a time, by application.

Both companies are run by the same founder, share the same engineering principles, and feed each other context. The agency work informs the open source. The open source attracts the agency clients. The SaaS products fund both.
